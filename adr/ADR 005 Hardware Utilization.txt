=====================================================================
ADR-005: Hardware Utilization
=====================================================================

Status: Accepted
Date: July 19, 2026

Context
-------
Piggy allows users to manually enter and manage expense information. No device
hardware is required to support the application's current functionality.

Decision
--------
No hardware features will be used.

Rationale
---------
- Expense information is entered manually.
- Camera and microphone are unnecessary.
- No additional device permissions are required.
- Simplifies development and testing.

Alternatives Considered
-----------------------
Camera
- Receipt scanning is outside the project scope.

Fingerprint Scanner
- Additional security is unnecessary for locally stored data.

Consequences
------------

Positive
- Simpler implementation.
- Easier testing.
- Improved user privacy.
- No permission requests.
- Reduced application complexity.

Negative
- No biometric authentication.
- Users must manually enter all expense information.
